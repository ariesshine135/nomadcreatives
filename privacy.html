Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nomad Creatives Corporation ("Nomad Creatives," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use FeedBecky, our design-feedback product, and our website at nomad-creatives.com (together, the "Service").

The short version
  • We collect only what we need to pull your design feedback into one place and keep it connected to what ships.
  • We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use your Google data to train AI models.
  • You can disconnect any integration at any time, and ask us to delete your account and data by contacting support.

1. Who this policy covers

FeedBecky is a business tool. If you use FeedBecky through your employer's workspace, your employer (our "Customer") controls that workspace and the content in it. In that case we process workspace content on the Customer's behalf and according to its instructions, and the Customer's own privacy practices also apply. For questions about workspace content, contact your workspace administrator first.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account and beta sign-up details: name, work email, company, role, and anything you include when you contact us.
  • Content you create in FeedBecky: comments, tags, priorities, resolutions, and decision-log entries.
  • Conversations with Becky: messages you send to Becky, FeedBecky's AI assistant, and voice audio if you talk to Becky during a session.

Information from services you connect

FeedBecky only accesses a third-party service after you choose to connect it, and only for the purposes described in this policy.

  • Google: see Section 4 for exactly what we access and how we handle it.
  • Figma: comments, comment authors, timestamps, and file and frame metadata for files you connect.
  • GitHub: repository, pull request, and issue metadata you choose to link to feedback.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and device data: pages and features used, log data, IP address, browser type, and error reports. We use this to run, secure, and improve the Service.
  • Cookies and analytics: we do not currently use analytics tools or advertising cookies. We may use cookies or similar browser storage that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and secure the Service. If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy first.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: collecting feedback from connected tools, organizing and prioritizing it, linking it to code, and generating summaries and decision logs.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To support you, respond to requests, and send service-related messages (for example, beta onboarding or security notices).
  • To monitor, debug, and improve the reliability and usability of the Service, using usage data that does not include the contents of your Google data.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms of Service.

We will only send you marketing email if you have opted in, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

4.2 How we use Google user data

We use Google user data only to provide and improve the user-facing features of FeedBecky described above. Specifically, we:

  • do not sell Google user data;
  • do not use or transfer Google user data for advertising, including retargeting, personalized, or interest-based ads;
  • do not use Google user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes;
  • do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models; and
  • do not allow humans to read Google user data unless (a) you give us affirmative consent for specific content, for example during a support request; (b) it is necessary for security purposes, such as investigating abuse; (c) it is necessary to comply with applicable law; or (d) the data has been aggregated and anonymized and is used for internal operations.

4.3 AI processing

FeedBecky uses third-party AI model providers to summarize feedback and extract action items. When we send content, including Google user data, to these providers, we do so only to generate output for you. We use providers whose commercial terms prohibit them from training their models on our customers' data. Current providers are listed in Section 6.

4.4 Sharing of Google user data

We share Google user data only (a) with members of your FeedBecky workspace, as part of the feature you are using; (b) with the service providers listed in Section 6 that help us run FeedBecky, under contracts that limit their use to providing services to us; (c) for security purposes, such as investigating abuse; (d) when required by law; or (e) as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, and only after obtaining your explicit prior consent.

4.5 Storage, retention, and deletion

Google user data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We keep it only as long as your account is active or you keep the integration connected. If you disconnect Google or ask us to delete your account, we delete the associated Google user data from our active systems within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days. You can also revoke FeedBecky's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4.6 Limited Use disclosure

FeedBecky's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How we share information generally

We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only:

  • within your workspace, with the teammates and administrators you work with;
  • with the service providers listed in Section 6;
  • to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights, safety, and security of our users, the public, or Nomad Creatives;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information; or
  • with your consent.

7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards appropriate to an early-stage service. These include encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, access controls based on least privilege, and secure storage of authentication tokens. No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident that affects your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

8. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Service. After that, we keep it only as long as needed to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Beta waitlist emails are deleted on request, or within 24 months if we have not onboarded you.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information. You may also have the right to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Deleting your account: to delete your FeedBecky account and its data, email dannielle@nomad-creatives.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account." We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within the timelines in Section 4.5.

To make any other request, email dannielle@nomad-creatives.com. We may need to verify your identity, and we will respond within the time required by law. If your data is part of a Customer workspace, we may refer your request to that Customer.

10. International users

We are based in the United States and process information there. If you use FeedBecky from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for those transfers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Children

FeedBecky is a business tool and is not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FeedBecky evolves. If we make material changes, including any change to how we use Google user data, we will notify you by email or in the product before the changes take effect, and we will ask for your consent where the law requires it.
