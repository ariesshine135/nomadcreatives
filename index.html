The problem 04 symptoms
  • Re-reading Slack channels comment by comment before every review
  • Scrubbing meeting transcripts for action items after the fact
  • Copy-pasting comments into a tracking doc, by hand, every time
  • Missing async feedback from stakeholders who weren't in the room

A designer prepping for a review does this manually, from scratch, every time. It's slow, it's error-prone, and it puts the burden of remembering on one person.

And because nobody has time to document what was decided, teams re-litigate ideas that were already explored, and leadership has no visibility into what was tried before something shipped.
